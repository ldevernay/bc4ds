April to May 2017   
ELOC Mobility is a digital service, designed to ensure safety on construction sites.   
Following the LCA, it appeared that most of the environmental impacts were due to the manufacturing and usage phases of the terminals.
The most impactful actions identified were :   
* Improved end-of-life management for terminals
* Refurbishment of terminals at the end of a project
* Replacement of the display terminal
* Change in business model
* Enhanced equipment intelligence to improve reliability
   
## Key figures
* Greenhouse gas emissions : -67%
* Water consumption : -75%
* Primary energy consumption : -80%
* Depletion of natural resources : -60%
