Some best practices were implemented : 
* Optimize models by focusing on domain-specific models
* Use efficient hardware
* Choose low-carbon data centers
* Implement sustainable prompt engineering techniques
* Usage tracking and rate limits
   
## Key figures
*  Pre-training Salesforce’s AI models resulted in 48 tCO2e, 11 times less than the emissions of GPT-3

## Beyond the numbers
* Energy conservation
* Increased efficiency
* Enhanced security
