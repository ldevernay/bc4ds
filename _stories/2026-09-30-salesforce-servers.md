Some best practices were implemented : 
* Implementing automation with Autonomous Control Plane (ACP)
* Process optimization
* Enhancing data management by disconnecting networks
* Safety measures
* Measuring Carbon Impact
   
## Key figures
* Energy conservation : saved 10,475 megawatt-hours of energy from April 2022 to July 2023
* Carbon emissions reduction: reduced 3,506 metric tons of carbon dioxide emissions
* Significant cost savings: the automation effort saved approximately $900,000 in energy costs alone between April 2022 and July 2023, while also reducing labor costs by decreasing the manual decommissioning process from 16 steps to just 3 steps - an 80% reduction in required manual intervention.


## Beyond the numbers
* Operational efficiency - increased decommissioning speed.
