In 2020, Sopra Steria worked on a B2C/B2B appointment scheduling solution.   
The first analysis led to:
- Better consideration of customer needs allowed to streamline operations, leading both to better customer experience and a lesser use of resources
- Optimisation of data stored
- No new systems architecture
  
## Key figures
Annual gain for 300 000 users:
- 4 200 L of water
- 30Kg CO2 emission saved
- 42 000 requests reduction
   
## Beyond the numbers
Better customer experience.
