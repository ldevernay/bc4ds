The integration of digital sustainability needed the organization of a dedicated team, training, monitoring and partnerships.  
The main actions taken, in ascending order of ROI : 
- Reduction in printing
- Extension of equipment lifespan by one year
- Decrease in server energy consumption
  
## Key figures
- 2 FTE
- 50k€ of annual budget
- 23 M€ savings per year
- 11,000 tons of CO2 equivalent saved / year

## Beyond the numbers
- Employee pride and talent attractiveness
