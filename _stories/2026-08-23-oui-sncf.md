Stronger ecodesign contraints to reduce HTTP requests, thus shortening the booking journey.

## Key figures

- 19% less CO₂ on the TER booking journey.
- A user journey 8 seconds faster.
- 300 KB fewer requests per booking, approximately 9 TB per year.

This helped raise awareness among teams.
