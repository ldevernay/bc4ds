In 2024, Ubisoft implemented the Power Plug solution to make sure unused devices go to sleep when not in use.  
It covers 12.000 devices across 32 countries and should soon go even farther.  
  
## Key figures
- 4,000 MWh energy savingsn which is equivalent to  kT CO2e.
- € 775.000 € in energy, saved every year.
