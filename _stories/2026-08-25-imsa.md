Starting from the RGESN, the iMSA implements sustainability on the digital services it produces.  
Their first case study is based on improving their website.  
In this white paper, they provide details on what they did, the results they obtained et what remains to be done.  

## Key figures

- -60% page weight
- -66% HTTP requests
- -15% GHG emissions

## Beyond the numbers

User Experience was improved, awareness of the stakeholdes was improved.
