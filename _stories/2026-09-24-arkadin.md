November 2017 to April 2018   
ArkadinVision is a videoconferencing solution.  
Following the LCA, two actions were identified : 
* Optimizing the cloud infrastructure of the ArkadinVision platform
* Supporting users to encourage the use of devices with a lower environmental impact
   
## Key figures
* Greenhouse gas emissions : -90%
* Water consumption : -80%
* Primary energy consumption : -80%
* Depletion of natural resources : -60%
